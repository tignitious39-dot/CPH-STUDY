🧮 Pharmaceutical Calculations

Dilution

C₁V₁ = C₂V₂

Where C₁ is the initial concentration, V₁ is the volume required from the stock, C₂ is the required concentration and V₂ is the final volume.

Percentage Strength

% w/v = grams of solute per 100 mL of solution.

% w/w = grams of solute per 100 g of preparation.

Ratio Strength

A ratio such as 1:1000 means 1 part of active substance in 1000 parts of the preparation.

Alligation

Alligation is used to determine the proportions of two different strengths required to prepare an intermediate strength.

Example

Prepare 100 mL of a 10% solution from a 25% stock solution.

C₁V₁=C₂V₂

25 × V₁ = 10 × 100

V₁ = 40 mL

Therefore use 40 mL of the 25% stock and add vehicle to make a final volume of 100 mL.
